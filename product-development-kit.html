Spectrometer Development Kit

Everything you need to evaluate SPIO micro optics and micro-spectrometer modules and integrate them into your own product - from first measurement to a prototype ready for volume production.

Overview

Everything you need in one box

The SPIO spectrometer development kit gives optical and photonic engineers a fast path to evaluating our chip-based spectrometers. It ships as a complete, ready-to-run set: a SPIO spectrometer module with detector attached, the adapter and flexprint to connect it, and a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B with power supply and micro HDMI cable.

Everything arrives with drivers and software pre-installed, so you can capture spectra on day one - no setup or integration work required to get started.

Use it to validate performance against your application, prototype an integration, and de-risk the move to volume production.
